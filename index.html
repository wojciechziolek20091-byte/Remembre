    Spending

    Every transaction
    Where the data comes from, and the rules Import a CSV, connect mBank, and edit the categories and budgets.

    Bring in transactions

    In mBank: Finanse → Historia → Lista operacji, then eksportuj listę and choose CSV. Importing the same file twice changes nothing, so overlapping exports are safe.

    Your bank

    Connect mBank once and new transactions arrive on their own, read-only. You will be sent to mBank to approve it; nothing here can move money. EU rules make you approve again every 180 days.

    Categories

    One category per line: the name, an equals sign, then the words to look for, separated by commas. The first line that matches wins, so put the specific ones above the general. Accents and capitals are ignored, so zabka finds ŻABKA.

    One per line: the category, an equals sign, and a limit in złoty. Leave a category out and it is simply not watched.

    One instalment per line: the day of the month, an equals sign, and the amount. Anything that arrives off this schedule is counted apart from it, and the app asks what it was for rather than guessing.

    What your spending says

    This runs on its own when you open this page, on the numbers already on your device – totals, the payees behind them and what repeats, never the whole statement. It is read again when the figures move, not every time you look.
