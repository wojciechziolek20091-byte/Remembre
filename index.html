    Reminders

    A “remember this” reminder the day before each task, at 17:00. Remembre has no server, so a reminder arrives while the app is open, or the next time you open it – it cannot wake your device on its own.

    Calendar alerts

    Hand your deadlines to your phone’s own calendar and it will alert you at 17:00 the day before, whether or not Remembre is open. A calendar file is a snapshot, so send it again when this says the calendar has fallen behind; entries update rather than duplicate. Turning on automatic syncing below replaces this with an address your calendar keeps up to date by itself.

    Automatic sync

    Pick a phrase and enter the same one on every device. Remembre then keeps them in step by itself, and gives your calendar app an address it can subscribe to once and never touch again.

    Anything you will remember and nobody would guess. It is the only key to your work, so it is never sent anywhere in readable form.

    Subscribe to this once in your calendar app and every deadline you add from now on turns up there on its own.

    Sync and backup

    A copy you keep yourself, and the way to move work between devices when syncing is off. Save it to iCloud Drive here, then load it on the other device. Loading merges – the newer version of each task wins and nothing is lost.

    Remembre keeps everything on your own device unless you turn on syncing. Press N at any time to add a task, or T to jump back to today.
